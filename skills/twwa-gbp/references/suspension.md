# Suspension risk and identity edits

- The profile name is the real business name (Impressum). No town or trade keywords added: high suspension risk and a guideline violation, even though it can rank.
- Address shown only where customers are received. Service-area businesses hide the address and set service areas (realistic, not the whole state). Virtual offices, c/o and post boxes are not allowed.
- One profile per real location; never one per town.
- Change the primary category only when it describes the core service; at most one identity-level edit per 48 h (name, address, pin, primary category, primary phone, website).
- After a suspension: no new profile; reinstatement request with evidence (business licence, shop photos, utility bill).
