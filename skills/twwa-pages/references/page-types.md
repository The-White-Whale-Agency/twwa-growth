# Page types by lead value (local services)

1. Service page per core service (money page): what, for whom, where (area with drive times), price range or how the price is built, process and duration, proof (own projects with photos and numbers), FAQ from real customer questions, phone and form above the fold.
2. Service × town: only with demand (gate A), a real service relationship (gate B) and ≥ 3 local evidence items (gate C); must fail the town-swap test (≥ 25 % of sentences town-bound). Otherwise it is a doorway page.
3. Cost page ("Was kostet …"): price drivers, real ranges from own jobs with date, what is included. Triggers AI Overviews often: explicit numbers win citations.
4. Case study / Referenz: one project, place (Ortsteil), problem, solution, materials, duration, photos with captions, owner quote with consent.
5. Problem pages ("Risse im Putz", "Dach undicht"): answer first, then when a professional is needed.
6. Funding pages (BAFA/KfW/BEG, Handwerk): only with a dated official source re-checked monthly (`evidence.py recheck`); rules changed on 2026-07-21 (iSFP bonus only above €30k).
German: Sie-Form unless `voice.address` says otherwise; regional terms (Gipser/Stuckateur); no filler, no superlatives without proof.
