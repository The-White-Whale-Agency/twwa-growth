# Reviews: German rules (summary, not legal advice)

- No incentive of any kind for a review: vouchers, discounts, raffles, gifts (court cases: OLG Frankfurt 2020, LG Hildesheim 2024). Google forbids it too.
- No gating: everyone is asked the same way, not only happy customers.
- No reviews by staff, family or as favours; no bought or written-for-them reviews.
- A review request by e-mail or messenger is advertising (BGH VI ZR 225/17). Whether the existing-customer exception (§7 Abs. 3 UWG) covers it is disputed: default to prior consent or ask in person / hand over a printed QR card. No reminders.
- §5b Abs. 3 UWG: where reviews are shown, state whether and how it is ensured they come from real customers.
- No star steering ("bitte 5 Sterne").
