# Prospect mode

- Read-only: GET requests, robots.txt respected, no form submissions, no accounts, no paid calls.
- Lead with one verified, concrete finding the owner can check in 10 seconds (e.g. "auf dem Handy nicht bedienbar", "in Google Maps unter Kategorie X statt Y", "Telefonnummer auf der Website anders als im Google-Profil").
- Never promise rankings, positions or numbers of Anfragen; never call a competitor bad; no "Nr. 1" or "garantiert".
- The report is a teaser: 5 points, no full roadmap; the roadmap is the paid onboarding.
