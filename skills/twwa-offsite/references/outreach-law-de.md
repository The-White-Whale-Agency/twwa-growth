# Outreach in Germany: who may send what (summary, not legal advice)

- E-mail, messenger and contact-form messages that promote the client need the recipient's prior express consent, also business to business (§ 7 Abs. 2 Nr. 2 UWG). Asking someone to list, mention or link the client promotes the client.
- So e-mail is fine where a relationship or an invitation exists: own membership (Innung, Kammer, Verband), existing business partner (manufacturer, supplier), a recipient that publicly invites suggestions (then use exactly their form or address), self-service directory sign-up.
- Press notes with real local news to an editorial address are common practice; keep them factual, no sales wording, include an unsubscribe line.
- Cold contacts (e.g. the author of a "best of" list who invites nothing): no e-mail. Use a short postal letter, or a phone call to the business where interest can be presumed (§ 7 Abs. 2 Nr. 1 UWG, B2B only).
- The agency sending on the client's behalf: needs a written mandate from the client (recorded in `config.outreach.mandate`), the sender must be identifiable ("Musterbetrieb, im Auftrag durch <Agentur>") with full signature, and every send is logged.
- Never: bought links without rel="sponsored", link exchanges, follow-up series, fake identities.
